# Tester guide

Thank you for helping. This takes about 10 minutes on your phone.

**What this is.** A student lab project that tries a different way to register for
courses: instead of racing to grab slots, you say what you want and the system works out
the timetables. It is **not** the real registration system. Nothing here registers you
for a real course.

**What you need.** The web address and the invite code you were sent. There is no password.

## Before you start

1. Open the address in your phone's browser.
2. Enter your invite code and tap **Sign in**.
3. You will see the courses already fixed for you (required and backlog). You only choose the rest.

The profile, credits, baskets and seat numbers are made up for this test. They will not
match your real semester.

## Scenario 1: a normal registration (about 3 minutes)

1. Tap **Next** to reach **Choose electives**.
2. Pick electives until the bar at the bottom shows 16–27 credits in green and says
   "… combinations available".
3. Tap **Next**. Skip the pin step by tapping **Next** again.
4. On **Rank timetable patterns**, put the patterns in the order you would like them. Drag
   the handle, or use the arrows. Tick "Never give me this" for any you would refuse.
5. Tap **Next**, look at the timetable preview, and tap **Submit**.
6. Fill in the short feedback form. This is the most useful part for us.

## Scenario 2: a registration with a slot pin (about 2 minutes)

1. Tap **Edit my choices**, then **Next** until you reach **Pin a slot**.
2. Choose a specific slot for one course, for example a morning slot for a theory course.
3. Watch the count at the bottom change: a pin always reduces the number of combinations.
4. Continue to the end and tap **Submit again**. Your new choices replace the old ones.

## Scenario 3: an impossible request (about 2 minutes)

We want you to see what happens when no timetable exists.

1. Tap **Edit my choices**. Add the elective **LAW2104** (International Relations) and
   **FRL1001** (Basic French) if you do not have them. Remove another elective if you go
   over 27 credits.
2. On the pin step, pin **FRL1001 theory** to **G1**.
3. The page should say no timetable is possible, name the two courses that clash, and list
   changes that would fix it.
4. Tap **Apply** on one of the suggestions and check that combinations come back.
5. Submit again if you want to keep the change.

If those two courses are fixed in your profile or the clash does not appear, try pinning
two different courses to slots that start with the same letter and number (for example
both to an `A1` slot).

## Afterwards

When registration closes, seats are allocated. Sign in again with the same code to see:

- the timetable you were given,
- a one-line reason (for example "Your 1st-choice pattern was full in …"),
- your place in the order and how the order was decided,
- one **add/drop** change, if you want to change your choices.

## How the order is decided

Students in the same priority tier are ordered by a published, reproducible method (the
equal-priority order). It does not depend on how early you submitted. If there is a later
round, anyone who missed their top pattern earlier is moved up.

## What we record

Your alias, the courses and patterns you choose, the time you reach each step, error
messages, and the feedback you type. No names, registration numbers or passwords. The
data is used only for this lab project and deleted when it ends. Questions or removal
requests: swapnil.23bcb7031@vitapstudent.ac.in

## If something breaks

Tell us what you tapped and what you saw, in the "What confused you?" box or by message.
A screenshot helps.
